# One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521.svg)](https://make.fun/c/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521)

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge](https://make.fun/c/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521):
one original line (under 200 characters) roasting a fridge, judged pairwise
by an AI comedy-club judge on win rate.

`main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) writes the chosen line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> My fridge holds twelve sauces and no food — less an appliance, more a museum of bad decisions.

94 characters, 96 bytes. See `BUILD_REPORT.md` for the 6 candidates
considered, their scores against the judge's rubric, and the
before/after of the tightening pass.
